Frequently asked questions

Why is my daily goal different from yesterday?

The goal is recalculated each morning based on your profile (weight, age, gender, activity level), the day's weather where you are, and the active minutes recorded by Apple Health or Health Connect. Hot or humid weather raises the goal. A long workout raises it further. If you didn't grant Health or Location permissions, the goal uses your climate preference as a fallback and is more stable day-to-day.

I'm not getting reminders

Check three things, in order:

  • Notifications: iOS Settings → Notifications → Water Reminder → Allow Notifications. On Android: Settings → Apps → Water Reminder → Notifications.
  • App-level toggle: Open Water Reminder → Settings → make sure "Reminders" is on.
  • Schedule: Reminders run hourly between your wake-up and sleep times. If your wake-up time is 9:00 and it's currently 8:30, the next reminder fires at 9:00.

iOS: Focus modes (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work) can silence reminders. Water Reminder uses time-sensitive notifications that respect your Focus settings — if you've allowed time-sensitive interruptions, they'll come through during Focus.

The app didn't add a bump for my workout

Two things to check:

  • Did you grant Apple Health (or Health Connect) permission during onboarding? You can re-grant it from Settings → Health.
  • The app reads "active minutes" from your activity rings / Health Connect — not raw step count. Walking generally counts; very light activity may not. Strenuous workouts log more active minutes and trigger larger bumps.

The bump is +25 ml per 10 active minutes. A 60-minute workout adds about 150 ml.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app permanently wipes all on-device data (profile, log history, streaks, preferences). To also delete the anonymous analytics profile we have on Mixpanel, email engineering@habuild.in with "Data deletion" in the subject. We action deletion within 30 days under GDPR / CCPA.

Can I export my history?

Not in version 1.0 — the app stores data on your device only. If we add iCloud / cloud export in a future version, we'll mention it in release notes and update the privacy policy.

Is my data private?

Profile and water logs live on your device. Anonymous usage events go to Mixpanel (EU region). We do not collect your email, phone number, precise location, or any payment information. Full details in the privacy policy.

I logged the wrong amount

You have 5 seconds to undo any log — a toast appears at the bottom of the home screen with an "Undo" button. After 5 seconds the log is committed; you can balance it by logging a custom amount (Settings → Custom amount → enter a negative-equivalent next time you log).

Does it work on iPad / Apple Watch / Android Wear?

iPad: yes (the app runs in portrait or landscape). Apple Watch and Wear OS: not yet — on the roadmap.

How do I report a bug?

Email engineering@habuild.in with:

  • What happened vs. what you expected
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Device model + OS version
  • App version (Settings → bottom of screen)
  • A screenshot or screen recording if relevant
